Knowledge base of the support assistant. Source: public content of
<https://www.albanandrieu.com> (English at the root, French under `/fr`).
Factual answers only; no commitment on timing, final price or action. When a
question is out of scope, point to "Talk to a human" or the right email address.

## How do I pay?

Page: `/payment`.

- By card, on Stripe's hosted checkout (one-time payment). The site never sees
  or stores card numbers: they are entered at Stripe.
- By SEPA bank transfer: the bank details are downloadable as a PDF on the
  payment page. Put the invoice or reference in the transfer label.
- Daily-rate payment: the `/checkout-tjm` page opens the Stripe payment
  (button or QR code).
- If Stripe checkout does not start: use the direct Stripe link on the payment
  page, a SEPA transfer, or write to job@albandrieu.com.

## I cancelled, or I am not sure my payment went through

- Cancel page: no payment was made; you can try again at any time.
- Status page: it says either that Stripe confirmed the payment or that
  verification is pending. When in doubt, check the email from Stripe, then
  write to job@albandrieu.com.
- The assistant cannot verify a payment, refund or change an invoice: those
  requests go to a human.

## How do I reach a human from the support page?

On `/support`, open "Talk to a human", write your message and, if you want a
reply, your email. The message and the last turns with the assistant are

openclaw@albandrieu.com.

## What happens to my support messages?

They are processed on the site owner's self-hosted server and are not kept by
the site. Do not share sensitive personal data, passwords or payment details.
For data questions, see the privacy policy (`/policy/privacy_policy`) or write
to contact@albandrieu.com.

## What is the Nabla homelab?

Pages: `/nabla`, `/truenas`, `/architecture`, `/workstation`. A TrueNAS Scale
homelab on one Mini-ITX machine (ZFS storage, self-hosted applications,
security tooling and AI experiments). It is used to test security and AI
integrations and to reproduce attacks targeting the owner's domains. The public
pages show a service catalog and public status that may be delayed or
incomplete: a "declared" service is not necessarily "observed" healthy. The
services themselves are not open to the public; the assistant gives no access,
credentials or internal addresses.

## Security and YubiKey guides

Pages: `/security`, `/security/yubikey`, `/security/dsomm`,
`/security/lexique`, `/security/ctem`, `/ciso`, `/ai`.

- The YubiKey guide explains what a security key is, why FIDO2 / WebAuthn
  matters, a two-key setup, recovery, then PIV, Vault PKI and Microsoft Entra
  certificate-based authentication. It is conceptual: no serial numbers, PINs
  or identifiers are published.
- DSOMM: explorer for the OWASP DevSecOps Maturity Model.
- Glossary: security, continuity, vulnerability and regulatory acronyms (BIA,
  RTO, RPO, SAST, DAST, CVSS, EPSS, KEV, DORA…).
- CTEM: Continuous Threat Exposure Management architecture.

The assistant does not provide personal hardware support (locked key, lost
PIN): contact the manufacturer or a human.
