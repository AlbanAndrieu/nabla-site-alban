Top #1 achievement

Security-first — anti-scraping, Keycloak identity and authorization, no friction for legitimate customers

Primary focus on security: protecting the business from abusive scraping and delivering robust identity and authorization with Keycloak—without slowing down or blocking legitimate customers, partners, or approved crawlers.

  • Scraping and abuse — Cloudflare WAF, rate limits, and bot controls tuned so real users and whitelisted SEO tools keep working while automated scraping drops sharply.
  • Keycloak — SSO, API gateway authentication for microservices, HA across regions, and policies (2FA, brute-force protection) aligned with the product experience.
  • Legitimate access — changes rolled out with monitoring so security hardening does not degrade trust or conversion for paying users.

Top #2 achievement

Migrating infrastructure to a state-of-the-art Kubernetes in the US with network refunding

US hosting and platform refresh: DNS, Kubernetes, and network modernisation—moving from Nomad to Kubernetes with cost optimisation and a clearer path for regional scale.

  • Kubernetes migration — assessment, cluster design, and rollout aligned with product availability and release cadence.
  • US footprint — hosting and connectivity choices that support latency, compliance, and operational ownership across teams.
  • Network — Magic WAN, VPC peering, VPN, and Cloudflare WARP so offices and cloud environments stay reachable and observable.

Top #3 achievement

Supporting business and tech through the transition to an AI company — hosting tuned legal models with deep reasoning and compliance checking, certified under ISO 42001

Platform work alongside product and GTM: reliable model hosting, guardrails that fit legal workflows, and governance that matches how regulated teams expect AI to behave in production.

  • AI platform foundations — Elasticsearch, GPU capacity, assistants, and document-classification services integrated with the rest of the stack and observability.
  • Legal-grade behaviour — tuned models and flows that emphasise deep reasoning and auditable checks rather than one-off demos.
  • ISO 42001 — first IA tech role implementing and maintaining ISO 27001, SOC II–aligned controls for AI systems, alongside existing security and privacy practice.

Security & compliance

Vulnerability management, IdP SSO, and certifications

Vulnerability & CVE reduction

  • Vulnerability remediation target: <7 days for critical CVEs; container scan pass rate >95%.
  • Vulnerability management: weekly vulnerability scanning and remediation of critical vulnerabilities. Using OpenVAS, Trivy and Semgrep.
  • Semgrep SAST, GitLeaks secret scanning, Mega-Linter and GitLab Ultima Sec for code security.
  • Implement Cloudflare WAF and DDoS protection for the website, rate limiting and bot detection.
  • Use Vault for secrets management, secret rotation, certificate automation and in transit encryption for user documents

IdP SSO with Keycloak

  • Keycloak authentication modernisation: migrated from legacy auth and upgraded through 4 major versions.
  • SSO integration with Microsoft Entra ID and other internal services; 2FA and brute-force protection enabled.
  • Keycloak in HA mode; token expiration and high-latency incidents resolved in production, and across multiple regions.
  • Use of Keycloak in API Gateway mode for authentication and authorization of microservices.

ISO, SOC & compliance

  • ISO 27001 preparation and support; Server hardening and firewall reviews.
  • Platform aligned with SOC II certifications achieved by Jusmundi.
  • First IA Tech implementing and maintaining ISO 42001 compliant security frameworks for IA.
  • Annual firewall reviews, access control, and EDR/XDR agents on new servers.
