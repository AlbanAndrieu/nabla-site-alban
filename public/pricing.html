Consulting pricing & engagement options

Below is how I usually structure work with startups and scale-ups. All amounts are quoted after a short scoping (ex. VAT / reverse charge per your status). A free 30-minute call is usually enough to produce a written estimate.

How pricing works

  • Day-based — most fractional and audit work is quoted in person-days or a monthly retainer tied to days per week.
  • Fixed sprint — short audits or well-bounded deliverables (e.g. “CI/CD hardening sprint”) can be a fixed package after a short discovery.
  • Expenses — cloud spend, travel or third-party tooling are billed at cost with prior agreement.
