Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-02-20

This policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies on this website, in line with the ePrivacy directive and the GDPR. For general data processing, see our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can be “first-party” (set by this site) or “third-party” (set by external services). They may be “strictly necessary” (required for the site to work), “functional” (e.g. preferences), or “analytics/marketing” (tracking, advertising).

2. Cookies we use

We use cookies only where necessary or with your consent:

  • Strictly necessary: e.g. cookie consent choice (so we remember your preferences), session or security-related cookies. These do not require consent under applicable law but we inform you about them here.
  • Functional: e.g. language or theme preference (if stored in a cookie). We aim to request consent for such uses where required.
  • Third-party: Google Translate and any analytics or other third-party scripts may set their own cookies. We use a consent management solution (e.g. Axeptio) so that non-essential cookies are only placed after you have agreed.

3. Consent and how to change your choices

When you first visit the site, you may see a cookie banner or similar prompt. You can accept all, reject non-essential, or choose by category. You can change your mind at any time by clicking the “Cookies” link in the footer, which reopens the consent options (where the openAxeptioCookies() function is available), or by adjusting your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Blocking all cookies may affect how the site works (e.g. language or theme may not be remembered).

4. Retention

Session cookies are removed when you close the browser. Persistent cookies are kept for the period stated in the consent tool or in the table provided there (e.g. 12 or 24 months for analytics). Consent itself is stored so we do not ask again until it expires or you clear it.

5. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy when we add or remove cookies or when the law changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised accordingly. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

For questions or to exercise your rights regarding personal data processed via cookies, see the Privacy Policy and the contact details given there.
